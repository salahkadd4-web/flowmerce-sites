# Google Slides–style resize handles, templates in the editor and personal templates — design

Date: 2026-11-21 · Branch: `feat/editeur-pro` (continued) · Builds on: `2026-11-20-edition-dans-la-page-et-modeles-design.md`

## Intent

The seller wants to resize images, buttons and sections **by dragging their edges or a corner, as in
Google Slides**. They also want to **choose a template from the editor**, and to keep **their own
design as a template** they can reuse.

The user left every decision to Claude: "settle everything for a better customer experience". Each
choice below is marked *(Claude's call)*.

## Goals

1. **Handles on the selected element:**
   - **Image** (banner, Image block, main product photo): handles on all 4 edges and in the corners.
   - **Button** (banner, product sheet, order form): width and height.
   - **Section:** height (bottom edge) and content width (side edges).
2. **While dragging:** a bubble shows the value. The value is written on release, as one history
   step, so Ctrl+Z undoes it. Arrow keys work on a focused handle.
3. **A "Modèles" button in the top bar** opens the template cards, with two groups:
   - Flowmerce templates;
   - "Mes modèles".

   Choosing one shows a confirmation and replaces the page; Ctrl+Z brings the previous page back.
4. **"Enregistrer comme modèle":** the seller names their page and keeps it as a personal template.
   Personal templates appear in the editor and in "Créer un site", and can be deleted.

## Non-goals

- Free positioning (dragging an element anywhere). Sections stay stacked: on a phone, free
  positioning breaks the page.
- Sharing templates between sellers.
- Automatic screenshots of personal templates. Their card is drawn from the page's first section:
  background photo or colour, plus the title.

## Anti-goals

- **A distorted photo.** Never.
- **A page that overflows on a phone after a resize.**
- **Breaking an existing page.** Every new setting is optional; without it, the page looks the same
  as before.
- **Replacing a page without warning,** or with no way back.

## Decisions *(Claude's call)*

- **Proportional sizes, not pixels,** so the same layout works on phone and computer:
  - image width: % of the section;
  - frame height: % of the image width;
  - button width: % of the content;
  - section height: % of the screen height.
- **Image:**
  - **Side edges:** width, from 30 to 100 %, in steps of 5. The image stays centred, so both sides
    move together.
  - **Top / bottom edge:** frame height (`imageHeight`), from 30 to 200 % of the width, in steps of 5.
    The photo fills the frame and is trimmed, never distorted (`object-fit: cover`).
  - **Corner:** width with the proportions kept.
  - **"Retour aux proportions"** in the image toolbar returns to the photo's natural frame
    (`imageHeight` null).
- **Button:**
  - **Side edges:** width — "auto" (fitted to the text), then 20 to 100 % of the content, in steps
    of 10.
  - **Top / bottom edge:** height through the 3 existing sizes (S / M / L), in steps.
  - **Corner:** both.
- **Section:**
  - **Bottom edge:** minimum height (`style.minHeight`): 0 (automatic), then 20 to 100 % of the
    screen height, in steps of 10. The content stays vertically centred.
  - **Side edges:** content width through the 3 existing widths (narrow / normal / wide).
  - Section handles are drawn through Puck's `componentOverlay` override, placed over the selected
    section.
- **Applying a template:**
  - **The template brings** the sections, photos, colours and fonts.
  - **The page keeps** its product (`productId`), its language, its tab title, and the shop name of
    its footer when the page had a footer.
  - **The confirmation reads:** "Votre page actuelle sera remplacée. Vous pourrez revenir en arrière
    avec Ctrl+Z."
- **Personal templates:**
  - stored in the database (`CustomTemplate`: tenant, name, content, date), at most 20 per seller;
  - name from 1 to 60 characters;
  - content checked by `validatePageContent` when saved and again when used;
  - in "Créer un site", the identifier `perso:<id>` is checked against the seller's ownership.

## Later: templates generated by AI (not built now)

The user is considering training an AI model on these templates, and generating templates from text
the seller types. Nothing is built for this now, but the data is ready for it:
- **Every template** (Flowmerce or personal) is a page **validated by the block schemas**. That makes
  a clean example for a model, and lets a generated template be checked before it is offered
  (`validatePageContent`).
- **A personal template also records its category** (`category`, free text, ≤ 40 characters, chosen
  from the Flowmerce categories or "Autre"). It is the label that will be needed to group examples
  or to ask for a category's template.

## Left to the builder

The exact look of the handles (Google Slides–style: small white squares with a blue border at the
corners, bars on the edges) and the copy of the windows, in French.
